Works/

Unconventional Conventions is a visual narrative of my journey through the Academy of Art University's School of Architecture—one that thrives on bending the rules while redefining them. This portfolio is both a reflection of my personality and an exploration of the unconventional, where the structured meets the unhinged, and the expected is disrupted by the unexpected. It captures the eccentricities of my design process, where diagrams, drawings, and visualizations become more than mere representations—they are fragments of thought, layered with individuality and curiosity. Through this collection, I challenge traditional conventions, embracing the beauty of controlled chaos and the artistry of breaking norms to forge my own design language.
